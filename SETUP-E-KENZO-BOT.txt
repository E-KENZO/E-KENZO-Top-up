E-KENZO Telegram Order Bot Setup

1) Create a Telegram bot
- Open Telegram and search @BotFather.
- Send /newbot.
- Choose a bot name, for example: E-KENZO Order Bot.
- Choose a username ending in Bot, for example: EKenzo_Order_Bot.
- BotFather gives you a BOT TOKEN. Keep it secret. Do not put it in index.html.

2) Deploy worker.js to Cloudflare Workers
- Create a Worker.
- Paste worker.js.
- Add secret/variable: TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN = your BotFather token.
- Add variable/secret: ADMIN_CHAT_ID = your numeric Telegram chat ID.

3) Get your numeric Telegram Chat ID
- After deploying, set the Telegram webhook to your Worker URL + /telegram/webhook.
- Open your new bot and send /start.
- The bot replies with your numeric Chat ID.
- Put that number into ADMIN_CHAT_ID and redeploy.

Webhook URL example:
https://api.telegram.org/botYOUR_BOT_TOKEN/setWebhook?url=https://YOUR-WORKER.workers.dev/telegram/webhook

4) Connect the website
- In E-KENZO-index.html, change:
  fetch("/api/order", ...)
  to your Worker API URL if the website and Worker are on different domains, for example:
  fetch("https://YOUR-WORKER.workers.dev/api/order", ...)

5) Important
- GitHub Pages alone cannot securely send a receipt directly to Telegram with a bot token.
- The bot token must stay on the Worker/server.
- The customer receipt is sent to the Telegram chat configured by ADMIN_CHAT_ID.
- The bot sends: Order ID, package, price, Roblox username, Telegram username, KHQR payment method, receipt image, and WAITING FOR ADMIN status.
- The bot does NOT mark payment as PAID automatically. Admin must verify the transaction.
